Weighted rates target: Eligible unique records in the selected Zotero collection; human reference and sampling class recognized; local input file available at selection time. They do not describe the original search pool.
Refusal and content-block counts require reviewing outcome_review.csv and rerunning summarize.
Zero confirmed refusals before adjudication does not establish absence of refusals.
Technical failures are not refusals. A missing label is not necessarily a content block.
IE is a targeted failure check, not validation of general extraction accuracy.
One paper remains one independent paper even when called three times per model.
Deployment differences and recurrence do not by themselves establish geopolitical bias.
Inspect deployment_audit.csv for the actual reported models/hosts; missing provider identity stays unknown.
Response-cache hits are not fresh repetitions and must not be scored as usable replicates.
